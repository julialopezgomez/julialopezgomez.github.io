Julia López Gómez

I am a first-year PhD student at the Edinburgh Centre for Robotics, part of the Centre for Doctoral Training in Deployable and Dependable Artificial Intelligence Robotics (CDT-D2AIR) within the University of Edinburgh and Heriot-Watt University. I recently graduated from the University of Edinburgh with an Integrated Master of Informatics (MInf), and I am excited to start my PhD journey in Robotics, Manipulation, and Machine Learning.

Research interests: robotics manipulation, motion planning, optimisation-based planning, and machine learning for robotics.

2023 - 2024 MINF1 Dissertation: Manipulation Planning with a Robotic Arm: A Tangent Configuration Space Approach · UoE
  • Formalized the use of a tangent parametrization of the robot configuration space (TC-space) in robot manipulation, leveraging the recent advancements of the C-IRIS algorithm in motion planning.
  • Proved the scalability of C-IRIS to robots with universal joints.
  • Utilized the Drake Robotics Toolbox for implementation and visualization.
  • Leveraged the mathematics of motion and manipulation planning, robot kinematics, algebraic rotations (SO(3) group), and optimization techniques.
  • Achieved a 1st Class mark.
  • Future Work - MINF2: Produce an algorithm to compute manipulation plans in the TC-space, contributing to the state-of-the-art in Robot Planning.
